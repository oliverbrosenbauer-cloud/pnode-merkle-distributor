A prototype for automatic pNode reward payouts: rewards computed off-chain are sealed into a Merkle tree anchored on-chain. One distributor account holds all the tokens; each operator claims their own share with a cryptographic proof — no individual transfers, no list maintained by hand, nothing that expires. Program used: saber merkle-distributor, the same design Jito forked for the JTO airdrop.

Not an official Xandeum product. An independent community prototype by a pNode operator, styled after the XAND staking portal. Runs on Solana devnet only, with worthless test tokens. The five demo wallets are embedded in this page together with their keys so anyone can click through without any setup — in real use each operator connects their own wallet, and only the wallet recorded in the tree can execute its claim.
